# My Favorite Movie: Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

![Captain America The Winter Soldier poster](poster.jpg)

## About

**Captain America: The Winter Soldier** is a *superhero spy-thriller* released in 2014 and directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. It is the second Captain America film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Steve Rogers struggles to adjust to the modern world while uncovering a conspiracy inside S.H.I.E.L.D.

> "I'm with you to the end of the line."
